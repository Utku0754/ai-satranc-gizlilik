AI Satranç — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 25 September 2026

Türkçe oku

AI Satranç does not collect your personal data. No account, no sign-in, and no information from you is required. To train the collective brain, only the moves of finished games are sent — not who you are.

1. Data That Stays on Your Device

The following is stored only on your own device and is not transmitted:

  • Your game archive and statistics
  • Your app settings
  • Your unfinished game

Deleting the app removes all of it from your device.

2. The “Collective Brain” Feature

All players raise a single AI together. For this, version 1.2 and later send the following to the app’s own server:

  • The moves and result of finished games. For example “e2e4, e7e5, … — White won”. Both the games you play and the training games the AI plays against itself are sent. Apart from the time the game reaches the server, nothing else is attached.
  • Training data (1.3 and later). For games the AI plays against itself in the Training tab or in the background: the positions, the AI's evaluation at each move and the game result. This is the same kind of training data Leela Chess Zero's volunteer computers send; it contains no personal information.
  • A randomly generated device number. Created by the app itself, it has no connection to your name, email, Apple/iCloud account or your device’s hardware identifiers. Its only purpose is to see whether a single device is influencing the collective brain disproportionately. Deleting and reinstalling the app generates a new one.
  • The AI’s weights (once). If your device has an AI that was trained before the collective brain moved to this system, its numerical weights are sent once as a “candidate”. The candidate is tested by playing games against the collective brain; if it is stronger, it becomes the collective brain. Weights are just a list of numbers and contain no personal information.

The collective brain is trained on these games and its new version is downloaded to everyone’s device. Without an internet connection, games wait on your device and are sent once you are online.

The data is stored on Vercel infrastructure (Vercel Inc., USA). Games are used only to train the AI; they are never sold, shared, or used for advertising or tracking. In version 1.1 and earlier, the collective brain used Apple’s CloudKit and sent only weight differences (no games); that path is closed with the update.

3. What Is Not Collected

  • Name, email, phone number or any other contact details
  • Location data
  • Contacts, photos or other app data
  • Advertising identifiers
  • Usage analytics
  • Your device’s hardware identifiers or your Apple/iCloud account

The app contains no advertising network, no analytics tool and no other third-party software. No user tracking is performed.

Because the games that are sent are not linked to your identity, we cannot tell which of them are yours; they therefore cannot be singled out and deleted per person.

4. Children

The app is suitable for all ages. No personal data is collected from anyone, including children; the game moves that are sent do not identify a person.

5. Changes

If this policy changes, this page will be updated and the date above will be revised.
