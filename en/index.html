AI Satranç does not collect your personal data. No account, no sign-in, and no information from you is required.

1. Data That Stays on Your Device

The following is stored only on your own device and is never transmitted:

  • The games you play and the moves you make
  • Your game archive and statistics
  • The AI’s learning history
  • Your app settings

Deleting the app removes all of it from your device.

2. The “Collective Brain” Feature (Optional)

The app includes an optional feature that merges your AI’s learning with that of other players. You can turn it off at any time under Progress → Join the collective brain. While it is off, the app runs entirely locally and sends no data at all.

While it is on, only these two things are sent:

  • The numerical difference of the neural network weights. This is a list of numbers describing how much the values in the AI’s brain changed. It contains no games, moves, text or personal information, and the games you played cannot be reconstructed from it.
  • A randomly generated installation number. Created the first time the app launches, it is unrelated to your identity, your device or your Apple account. Its only purpose is to keep a single device from influencing the shared model disproportionately. Deleting and reinstalling the app generates a new one.

This data is stored in Apple’s CloudKit infrastructure. No separate server is used.

3. What Is Not Collected

  • Name, email, phone number or any other contact details
  • Location data
  • Contacts, photos or other app data
  • Advertising identifiers
  • Usage analytics

The app contains no advertising network, no analytics tool and no other third-party software. No user tracking is performed.

4. Children

The app is suitable for all ages and collects no personal data from children either.

5. Changes

If this policy changes, this page will be updated and the date above will be revised.
