Frequently Asked Questions

Why does the AI play so badly?

On purpose. AI Satranç ships with no chess engine: the AI's neural network is born with random numbers on first launch and only learns by playing. Its clumsy early games are proof that learning really starts from zero.

How do I make it learn faster?

Open the Training tab and tap Start Training. The AI plays against itself and learns after every game — on a Mac this is the fastest way. It also learns from every game you play against it.

How do I know it is really learning?

The Progress tab shows learning curves, training steps and match-test results. At regular intervals the new brain plays a tournament against its older self; if it has clearly gotten worse, the learning is rolled back.

I see "Learning is unavailable on this device".

On-device training uses the Metal GPU. Where Metal isn't supported (for example the iOS Simulator), the app can be played but does not learn.

What is the "shared brain"? Is my data shared?

When this optional feature is on, only a numeric summary of learning (a weight difference) and a random install number are shared. Your games, moves and personal data never leave your device. Turn it off under Settings → Join the shared brain (in version 1.0: at the bottom of the Progress tab). See the privacy policy for details.

Can I reset the AI?

Deleting and reinstalling the app fully resets the brain, archive and statistics — the AI is born random again.

Is there an account, ads or in-app purchases?

No. No account, sign-in, ads, analytics or in-app purchases. Everything works offline.
